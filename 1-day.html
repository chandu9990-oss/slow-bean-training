1-Day Intensive

₹12,000 · 1 Day (8–9 hours)

A refined introduction to the world of bean-to-bar chocolate. Perfect for beginners and those seeking a focused first experience with the craft.

  • Overview of grinding, refining, and tempering
  • Guided tasting session
  • Factory visit

Inclusions

  • Factory visit
  • Lunch and refreshments
  • Certificate of Participation
  • Recipe notes and reference materials
