Factory & Farm Visits

Connecting the craft to its origins and production realities.

Factory Visit

Included in all programmes (1-day, 3-day, and 7-day).

  • Guided tour of a working bean-to-bar facility
  • Observe industrial-scale roasting, refining, conching, and tempering
  • Learn about quality control, packaging, and production workflows
  • Q&A with the production team

Farm Visit

Included in the 3-day and 7-day programmes.

  • Visit to a cacao farm to understand cultivation practices
  • Learn about harvesting, fermentation, and drying processes
  • Meet farmers and understand the origin story of the cacao
  • Taste fresh cacao pulp and observe post-harvest handling

These visits are an integral part of the learning experience, providing context that classroom training alone cannot offer.
